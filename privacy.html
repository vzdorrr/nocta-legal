Nocta Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026.

Nocta is an app for chatting with characters voiced by a language model that runs entirely on your phone. The developer operates no servers that receive your data, and the app contains no ads, analytics or third-party trackers.

What is stored on your device

  • Chats, photos you send in a chat, character profiles, companion memory and settings.
  • Downloaded language models.

All of this stays in the app's storage on your phone. Cloud backup of app data is disabled. The developer cannot see or receive this data.

When the app uses the internet

  • Model downloads. Models are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Hugging Face sees your IP address and which file you download. The Hugging Face privacy policy applies.
  • Web search (only if you turn this tool on for a chat). The search query written by the model is sent to DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia or Brave Search (if you entered your own Brave key). These services see the query and your IP address. Your chat as a whole is not sent.
  • Voice input uses your phone's system speech recognition service (for example, Google on Android or Apple on iPhone). Audio may be processed by that service under its own terms. If you don't use the microphone, no audio is recorded.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only for voice input, when you tap the button.
  • Notifications — to tell you about new replies and to show model download progress.
  • Photos are picked through the system picker; the app only receives the files you choose.
  • Camera — only to take a photo for a chat, when you tap the button.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android) — so a character can reply or write first while the app is closed. This permission gives the app no data.

Reports and emails to the developer

When you report a reply or contact the developer, your email app opens with a prepared message. It is sent only if you send it yourself, and it contains only what you see in the draft (for example, the reply text and the model name). Such emails are used only to review the report and answer you.

Children

The app is intended only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect children's data — and we do not collect personal data at all.

Deleting your data

Delete a chat or a model in the app, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app from your phone (Android or iPhone) — everything is deleted with it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new date.
